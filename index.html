About

I am a final-year Biomedical Engineering student at KPR Institute of Engineering and Technology (KPRIET), Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India, ranked 2nd in my batch. I work as a Trainee-R&D at Cogniaxis AI Technologies Pvt. Ltd., on a surgical intelligence platform, and I do research on coronary artery segmentation under Dr. Haseeb Hassan.

At KPRIET I am President of the BMESI chapter and Technical Lead of the IEEE EMBS Student Chapter, two separate societies.

In the National Cadet Corps (NCC) I am a Cadet Warrant Officer with 2 TN Air Squadron NCC. As the Best Cadet (Senior Division, Air Wing) of the Tamil Nadu, Puducherry & Andaman and Nicobar Directorate, I represented the directorate at the Annual NCC Republic Day Camp in New Delhi (29 December 2024 to 29 January 2025) and placed 4th nationally in the All-India Best Cadet Competition.
